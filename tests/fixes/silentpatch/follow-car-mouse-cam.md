# In-car camera mouse behaviour

Group `follow-car-mouse-cam` (mid_hook at 0x525615).

**Bug.** Moving the mouse up and down while looking left, right or behind in a vehicle tilts the camera.

**Port.** Mouse pitch applies only with mouse steering of the 3rd-person camera on and the look direction forward
(3).

**Reproduction.** Not scripted yet.

**Result.** Lifted and converted; no reproduction yet.
