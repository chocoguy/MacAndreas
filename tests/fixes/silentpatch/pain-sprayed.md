# PAIN_SPRAYED

Group `pain-sprayed` (mid_hook at 0x4b3349).

**Bug.** Peds sprayed with a spray can or an extinguisher, or choking on tear gas, use the generic
pain sound.

**Port.** As SilentPatch: those weapons make the ped say PAIN_SPRAYED, unless the attacker is
pistol-whipping with the can, and the damage response returns through its own epilogue.

**Result.** Lifted and converted.
